EPISODE 158

“Hai ragione?”

Grammar Focus: 22.2 - Uses of avere
CEFR: B1

Episode job

Deepen avere beyond possession into everyday idiomatic thought, need, stress, and judgment.

Logline

A family dispute about responsibility becomes a showcase for how often people use avere to
talk about needs, opinions, excuses, and emotional limits without ever really talking about
“having” anything at all.

A-Story

Luca starts noticing how widely avere functions across ordinary family speech.

B-Story

Elena and Matteo argue over who has reason, who has time, and who has the patience to
continue the discussion.

C-Story

Lidia tries to lower the temperature by reframing what people actually “have” in this moment:
stress, not solutions.

Core language target

● avere ragione
● avere torto
● avere bisogno
● avere voglia
● avere fretta
● avere paura
● avere tempo
● avere pazienza

Story beats

● A practical issue turns into a relational argument.
● Luca hears avere repeated across emotional, practical, and judgment-based uses.
● Matteo uses it socially; Elena uses it diagnostically.
● Pietro insists that “having reason” is not the same as being useful.
● Luca starts hearing avere as a core carrier of adult-life language.

Emotional movement

Luca becomes more fluent in the family’s everyday emotional shorthand.

Writer notes

● Strong for emotionally loaded but familiar family conflict.
● Keep the stakes ordinary.
● Elena and Matteo are the best engine here.
